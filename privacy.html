Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Blunt Discord bot and website collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. We try to collect as little as possible.

1. Information we collect

Server configuration data. To make features work, Blunt stores identifiers and settings that server admins create, such as:

  • server (guild), channel, role and message IDs;
  • settings like prefix, welcome and leave messages, autorole, log channel, anti-nuke settings and trusted users;
  • autoresponder triggers and replies, sticky messages, honeypot and partnership settings;
  • saved voice channel for auto-rejoin, temporary roles, and servers blocked from using the bot;
  • levels/XP and giveaway data, linked to Discord user IDs.

Member events. Blunt receives member join, leave and update events so it can send welcome and leave messages, assign autoroles, apply and remove temporary roles and bans, and run anti-nuke protection. These events are used as they happen and are not stored beyond the settings and records described in this policy.

Deleted and edited messages. For the snipe and esnipe commands, Blunt keeps a short history of recently deleted or edited messages in a channel (author, content and time) in its database. Entries are kept for up to 7 days, with at most 100 entries per channel, and older entries are removed automatically. Server moderators can clear a channel's history with the cs command.

Other short-lived data. Your AFK status and reason, and moderation records the bot needs to do its job (for example jail or temporary role information), are stored until they end or are removed.

Message content. Blunt reads messages in servers it is in so it can run prefix commands, automod, autoresponders, sticky messages, autothread and image-only channels, starboard and the honeypot. It does not keep a general log of everything people say, and message content is not used to train machine learning or AI models.

AI requests. When you use an AI or translate command, the text you send for that command is passed to a third-party AI provider to produce an answer.

Website. The website stores your music on/off and volume choice in your own browser (local storage). It loads live bot statistics from our bot server. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use it

  • to run the bot's commands and features in your server;
  • to protect servers (anti-nuke, honeypot, moderation actions);
  • to keep the bot working, fix bugs and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Discord, since the bot runs on Discord's platform.
  • Database providers that store the bot's settings.
  • Third-party AI services that receive the text of AI or translate requests.

The bot and website run on our own VPS. These services have their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

Server settings are kept while the bot is in your server, and can be reset or deleted on request. Snipe history is kept for up to 7 days and at most 100 entries per channel. If the bot is removed from a server, its saved settings and data are deleted automatically. You can also ask us to delete data at any time.

5. Your choices

  • Server admins can change or remove most stored settings with the bot's commands, or remove the bot at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete data linked to your Discord user ID or your server ID by contacting us in the Support Server.
  • You can avoid AI processing by not using the AI and translate commands.

6. Security

We keep tokens and database credentials private and use reputable providers. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Blunt is meant for people who are old enough to use Discord under Discord's own rules. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If you keep using Blunt after a change, you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

For questions or data requests, join the Support Server and open a ticket or message a staff member.

Blunt is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord.
