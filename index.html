What Blunt does

The tools that keep a server safe and tidy, without the clutter.

Moderation that works

Kick, ban, timeout, jail, purge, lockdown and slowmode. Use prefix or slash commands, whichever you like.

Anti-nuke shield

If someone starts mass-deleting channels or banning people, Blunt steps in right away. You choose the limits and who you trust.

Welcome and goodbye

Write your own welcome and leave messages, preview them privately first, and start over whenever you like.

Socials lookup

Look up Instagram, TikTok, GitHub, Snapchat, Roblox and Letterboxd profiles without leaving Discord.

Music

Play songs from YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify links, with a queue, shuffle, loop and volume control.

Handy utilities

Steal emojis, snipe deleted messages, go AFK, set autoroles, sticky messages and autoresponders.
